<objective>
Wire the noindex field end-to-end: sync module support (types + parser + repository), generateMeta robots emission, and apply noindex to the three affected EN placeholder posts.

Purpose: Placeholder EN posts (guia-eeat.en, sql-vs-nosql.en, experiencia-de-usuario EN route) are thin content that harms crawl budget and risks soft-404 signals. The noindex field already exists in CMS (added by plugin-seo) and is checked by the sitemap endpoint, but generateMeta never emits the robots directive to the browser — so Googlebot indexes the pages despite the CMS flag.

Output: Four source files updated (types, postParser, payloadRepository, generateMeta) + one new EN stub markdown file. After this plan, a sync push of any file with noindex: true will write the CMS flag, and generateMeta will emit the correct robots directive.
</objective>
