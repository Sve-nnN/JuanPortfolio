## Notes on the Spanish SEO Landscape for This Niche

The Spanish-language SEO and software engineering keyword space is small by global standards. Most target keywords have volumes of 100–800/mo in Spain, KD (Keyword Difficulty) of 0–22, and high traffic potential relative to their volume (indicating thin competition). This is an opportunity: a new site with modest authority can rank for nearly every primary keyword if the content and on-page signals are correct. Low-volume niches reward topical depth over link-building scale.
